Fiduciary

This tool illustrates how DST return mechanics work. It is not investment, tax, or legal advice. Its tax figures are rough estimates: it models federal capital-gains tax and, optionally, unrecaptured Section 1250 depreciation recapture, both at rates you supply. It does not model state or local tax, the 3.8% net investment income tax, passive-activity or at-risk limits, distribution timing, sponsor-specific fees beyond the two modeled here, or the terms of any actual offering.

The default per-category return figures are illustrative placeholders, not researched market data and not a forecast. Consult a qualified tax advisor and read the offering documents before making any investment decision. Nothing here carries any guarantee of accuracy.
